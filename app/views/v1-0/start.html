Access cattle vaccination and testing data

This is a service for veterinarians invited to join the bovine tuberculosis (bovine TB) vaccination scheme.

Use this service if you are:

  • a private veterinarian
  • an Official Veterinarian (OV)
  • an approved tuberculin tester
  • and have been invited by Defra to vaccinate or test cattle

You can use this service to:

  • prepare a list of cattle to vaccinate or test for bovine TB
  • report vaccinations you have completed
  • report test results back to Defra
  • before or after a farm visit

Before you start

You will need the county parish holding (CPH) number, or an ear tag number for the herd or animal you are visiting.
