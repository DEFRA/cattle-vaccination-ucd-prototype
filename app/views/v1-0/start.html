Prepare and do farm visits

Use this service to prepare cattle lists for farm visits and manage related work.

You can use this service to:

  • prepare a list for vaccination, TB testing or both
  • view open work orders
  • report completed work after a visit

Before you start

You need the county parish holding number (CPH) or an ear tag for the herd or animal you want to work with.
