Vaccinating and testing cattle for TB

Use this service to prepare for, carry out and report bovine tuberculosis (TB) vaccination and testing activities in England.

The service is for Official Veterinarians (OVs), Approved TB Testers (ATTs) and authorised practice staff involved in TB testing and vaccination.

Using this service you can:

  • prepare for TB testing or vaccination visits
  • view herd and animal information
  • record vaccination and test results
  • submit results to the Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA)
  • manage and track your testing activity

Before you start

You must be authorised to carry out bovine TB testing or vaccination. This usually means holding the appropriate Official Veterinarian (OV) authorisation or being registered as an Approved TB Tester (ATT).

Make sure you have your sign-in details and access to your organisation’s account before starting.
