V1

Vaccination – prepare a list and reporting

The first version of the service. It covers the full vaccination journey, from finding a farm to submitting a report.

What you can do in V1

Sign in

Sign in to the service using GOV.UK One Login. There is a placeholder one-login email and password flow.

Find a farm

Search for a herd or animal by county parish holding (CPH) number or ear tag number. A search results page shows the matching farms; you select the one you want to visit and confirm the details before continuing.

Prepare a list of cattle to vaccinate

Once you have chosen a farm, you can prepare a printable list of every animal on the holding so you can take it with you on the visit.

Record vaccinations during the visit

Record who gave the vaccine, the vaccination date, vaccine batch details and diluent batch details. Tick the cattle that were vaccinated and mark any that were not, with a reason for each.

Submit a vaccination report

Review the answers before sending the report to APHA. After submission you see a confirmation page summarising what was reported.

Plan future visits

You can schedule the next vaccination visit for a farm and see a confirmation of the booking.

Not in this version

V1 does not include the bovine TB skin test journey. That was added in V2.
