v1-7 – UX notes

Screen by screen: purpose, elements, where data comes from, interaction rules, validation, data saved and accessibility. Pink boxes are prototype shortcuts or made-up data, not design decisions. Amber text is for you to fill in.

1 – Guidance

/vax/v1-7/1-guidance · Reached from “Start now” on the prototype home page

Purpose

Tells the vet what the service is for and who should use it, before they log in.

Elements

  • Heading “Vaccinate cattle for bovine TB” and a one-line description of the service.
  • “Use this service if you are:” – who can use it (OV or qualified APHA officer, approved tuberculosis vaccinator).
  • “You can use this service to:” – the three things it does.
  • “Continue to log in” button.
  • “Contact us for help” details.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • No Back link – it is the first screen.
  • “Continue to log in” goes to Log in (2).
  • The header has no “My tasks” link here (not logged in yet); the service name goes back to this page.

Validation

None.

Data saved

Nothing.

Accessibility

To be worked out.

/vax/v1-7/2-log-in

Signs the vet in. In the prototype it only captures their name.

Elements

3 – My tasks

/vax/v1-7/3-my-tasks · Also the “My tasks” link in the header

Purpose

Lists the vaccination tasks assigned to the vet, so they can start, continue or view one.

Elements

  • Heading “[name]’s task list”.
  • One summary card per task. Title: farm name, then “TB task number [number]” underneath.
  • Card action: “Start work”, “Continue work” or “View work”.
  • Card rows: Task type, CPH number, Post code, Task created, Booking date, Status. When complete: Complete date instead of Task created and Booking date.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

Task stateActionStatusWhen
To doStart workBlue “To do” tagNothing done yet.
In progressContinue workGreen “In progress” tagA vaccination list made, or a report started or submitted.
CompleteView work“Complete” (plain text)The latest report was complete, or the vet closed the task. Complete date is the day that happened.
  • Completed tasks move to the bottom of the list.
  • Every action link goes to the CPH task list (4).
  • The card action stays on one line; long titles wrap instead.
Prototype: two hard-coded tasks. Only Pinecone Grove Farm (VACTB-104523) changes state; Hill farm (VACTB-104871) is always “To do” and also opens Pinecone Grove’s task list. Task created, booking date and post codes are example values.

Validation

None.

Data saved

Nothing – it reads the state of the task.

Accessibility

To be worked out. (Each action link has hidden text naming its farm and task number.)

4 – CPH task list

/vax/v1-7/4-cph-task-list

Purpose

The task’s hub: farm details, preparing the vaccination list, reporting, the reports made and closing the task.

Elements

  • Back link “Back to ‘My tasks’”.
  • Caption: farm name. Heading “TB vaccination task number [number]”.
  • CPH information: CPH number, keeper name, contact number, email address, address.
  • Prepare for the farm visit: 4 numbered steps, “You can refresh the vaccination list if needed.”, “Create vaccination list” button.
  • “Important” banner when the list is still up to date.
  • One section per list, newest first, headed “Vaccination list: [date]”.
  • Close work order section with a “Close task” button.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

Create vaccination list

  • Makes a new list dated today, unless nothing on it would change – then no list is made and the “Important – The latest vaccination list is still up to date” banner shows.
  • Exception: once the latest list has been reported on, a new list is always made (a reported list can’t be downloaded).
  • If the latest list was made today and hasn’t been reported on, the new list replaces it (so two lists never share a heading). On another day, both stay.
  • After creating, focus and scroll move to the new list’s download link (or to the banner).

A list not yet reported on

  • “Download vaccination list (opens in a new tab)” – grey “Ready” tag. Opens the printable list as it was when made.
  • “Report vaccination status” – newest list only. Hint “[N] cattle to report”: before any report, every animal on the list (the vet decides about ineligible ones); after a report, what that report left.
  • Status: blue “To do”, or green “In progress” once the report has started.
  • The first report goes straight to the vaccination date (5). Later reports start afresh: answers cleared, cattle already reported carried as reported.

A list reported on

  • “Report vaccination status” – “Completed”, hint “[N] cattle reported, view report for details” and, if any are left, “[N] cattle still need reporting”.
  • “View vaccination report [CPH] [date] (opens in a new tab)” – green “Ready to view”, hint “The keeper was sent a copy via email”. Opens the vaccinated cattle report.

Close work order

  • Shown once a report has been submitted.
  • “Close task” marks the task complete (today’s date) and goes to My tasks.
Prototype: keeper name, phone and email are made up. “View work” on a completed task still shows this page with nothing to say it’s closed.

Validation

None.

Data saved

  • Each list – date, every animal’s note at that moment, cattle to report, and the report made against it – vax-lists.
  • Task closed date – vax-task-closed.

Accessibility

To be worked out.

5 – Vaccination date

/vax/v1-7/5-vaccination-date

Purpose

When the cattle were vaccinated – the date of the visit being reported.

Elements

  • Caption “Report TB vaccination for [farm]”, heading “What was the vaccination date?”.
  • Date input (Day, Month, Year), hint “For example, 20 4 2028”.
  • “Continue and save”, “Save and continue later”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • Continue goes to Vaccine batch (6). From check your answers (Change), it goes back there, and Back does too.
  • Save and continue later goes to the CPH task list.
  • Accepts a 2-digit year (26 = 2026) and the month as a number or a name (Oct, October, Sept – any case).

Validation

  • Any part empty: You must enter a date
  • Not a real date (31 2 2026, letters, a 3-digit year): You must enter a valid date, for example 12 4 2027
  • After today (today is fine): The vaccination date must be in the past

Data saved

  • vaccination-date-day, -month, -year.

Accessibility

To be worked out.

/vax/v1-7/6-vaccine-batch

The vaccine and diluent used.

Elements

  • Heading “Enter vaccine batch details”.
  • Vaccine batch number (hint “For example, 125007G”), vaccine expiry date (month and year), diluent batch number, diluent expiry date.
  • “Continue and save”, “Save and continue later”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • One batch only.
  • Continue goes to the reasons screen (7); from check your answers, back there.

Validation

  • Batch number empty: You must enter a vaccine batch number (and the diluent equivalent).
  • Expiry empty: You must enter a vaccine expiry date
  • Expiry not a real month and year: You must enter a valid vaccine expiry date, for example 4 2027
  • Expiry before this month: The vaccine expiry date must be in the future
  • The same three rules for the diluent expiry date.

Data saved

  • The batch – vaccine-batches (batch number, expiry month and year, diluent number, diluent month and year).

  • “Or”, then “No, all presented cattle were vaccinated”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • “No, all presented cattle were vaccinated” is exclusive: ticking it clears the reasons, and ticking a reason clears it.
  • With reasons: on to one select screen per reason (8), in this order.
  • With “No, all presented…”: straight to screen 9.
  • Coming back and picking reasons after an earlier “all vaccinated” clears the cattle already marked as vaccinated.

Validation

Data saved

8 – Select all cattle [reason]

/vax/v1-7/8-select-cattle/[reason] · one screen per reason ticked on 7

Purpose

Pick the cattle that weren’t vaccinated for this reason.

Elements

  • Heading from the reason, e.g. “Select all cattle for slaughter in the near future”, “Select all cattle not at location”.
  • “Selected animals [n] of [herd size]”, “Selected cattle are saved automatically as you progress.”
  • “Search for an animal” – “Enter the last 4 digits of the official ear tag” (autocomplete).
  • Pagination (top and bottom). Each screen shows three pages of the printed list: “Page N of M — cattle range 0013 - 0076”, “Select all cattle on page N”, a table (No., Select ear tag number, Notes, Date of birth, Sex, Breed).
  • “Continue and save”, “Save and continue later”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • Pages match the printed list: 35 cattle on page 1, 40 on each after, in the list’s order (last 4 digits of the ear tag).
  • A heavy line after every 5th animal; shared last 4 digits have a bar above and below.
  • Ticks are saved when moving page, searching, continuing or saving for later.
  • Cattle already given another reason (or reported in an earlier report) are shown ticked, greyed and locked, with that reason in Notes (e.g. “Intended for export”, “Too young”).
  • “Select all cattle on page N” ticks every unlocked animal on that page.
  • The count is out of the whole herd and includes cattle already ticked under any reason.
  • Search: type the last 4 digits, pick from the list – that animal is ticked and its page opens focused on its row.
  • The search label and box stick to the top while scrolling (the “Search for an animal” heading scrolls away).
  • Continue: the next reason’s screen, then 9. If the reasons cover every animal, straight to 10.
  • On a phone each row stacks, with labels.

Validation

None – the vet can continue with no cattle ticked.

Data saved

  • The cattle per reason – vax-selected-cattle.

Accessibility

To be worked out. (The count is a live region; a locked animal’s reason is linked to its checkbox.)

11 – Add a note about an animal

/vax/v1-7/11-add-note · MoJ “Add another” component

Purpose

Record a note against one or more animals on the list.

Elements

  • Per note: “Note 1” (or “Note 1 of 2”), “Search for an animal” – “Enter the last 4 digits of the ear tag number” (autocomplete).
  • Once an animal is picked: Animal details (Date of birth, Sex, Breed, Last vaccination date) and “Enter a note for this animal”.
  • “Remove note N” under each note when there is more than one, with a line after it.
  • “Add another note”, “Continue and save”, “Save and continue later”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • Search: up to 4 digits matches the last 4 of the tag; more digits match anywhere in the whole number (e.g. editing a picked tag). Picking fills the box with the full tag and shows the details and note box.
  • Typing over a picked tag clears the animal until another is picked.
  • Last vaccination date: this report’s date if the animal is reported vaccinated in it, else an earlier report’s, else its record, else “Not vaccinated”.
  • Add another note copies the first note, empty. Remove takes that note away and renumbers the rest; focus then moves to the page heading (MoJ’s behaviour).
  • Continue goes to 12.

Validation

  • Search empty or under 4 digits: Search for an animal by entering the 4 last digits of the official ear tag
  • Digits match no animal: No animal on the list has an ear tag ending in [digits]
  • Digits match more than one and none picked: More than one animal ends in [digits] – choose one from the list
  • Note empty: You must enter a note

Data saved

  • The notes – vax-notes (animal, note).

Accessibility

To be worked out.

13 – Add the cattle that were not on the vaccination list

/vax/v1-7/13-enter-unexpected-cattle · MoJ “Add another” component

Purpose

Record each animal found that wasn’t on the list, and any that couldn’t be identified.

Elements

  • Per animal: “Animal 1” (or “Animal 1 of 2”), “Search for an animal” – “Enter the official ear tag number” and a Search button.
  • Once found: Animal details (Date of birth, Sex, Breed, Last vaccination date), “Was this animal vaccinated?” Yes / No. No reveals “Why wasn’t the animal vaccinated?”: For slaughter in the near future, Intended for export, Intended for germplasm production, Keeper declined vaccination, Clinical condition prevents vaccination, Animal is deceased, Vaccinated recently, Too young to vaccinate.
  • “Enter a note for this animal (optional)”.
  • “Remove animal N”, “Add another animal”.
  • Unidentified cattle: “Some cattle could not be identified” checkbox, revealing “How many cattle could not be identified?”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • Search (or Enter in the box) looks the tag up without leaving the page. Spaces don’t matter; “UK” is added if left off.
  • Add another animal copies the first animal, empty; Remove takes one away and renumbers; focus moves to the page heading after a Remove (MoJ).
  • With unidentified cattle ticked and a number, the vet can continue without entering any animal.
  • Continue goes to check your answers; saving here also records Yes on 12.
Prototype: a tag is “found” if it belongs to an animal on one of the prototype’s other farms (Tarbh, Hill, Mill House…), with that animal’s details. Pinecone Grove’s own tags aren’t found (they’re on the list). Some farms record “Date not recorded” for the last vaccination.

Validation

  • Tag empty or not found: You must enter a valid ear tag number
  • Found but no answer to “Was this animal vaccinated?”: Select ‘Yes’ if the animal was vaccinated
  • Unidentified ticked without a whole number of 1 or more: You must enter a number

Data saved

  • The animals – vax-unexpected (tag, vaccinated, reason, note).
  • vax-unidentified and vax-unidentified-count.

Accessibility

To be worked out.

14 – Check your answers before submitting vaccination details

/vax/v1-7/14-check-answers

Purpose

Review everything – including cattle with no answer – before submitting.

Elements

  • Vaccine details: Date of vaccination, Is the job complete?, batch number, batch expiry, diluent number, diluent expiry.
  • Vaccinated cattle: Vaccinated cattle [n], Ineligible cattle [n].
  • Every animal on the list, page by page (“Page N of M — cattle range …”), with its row number, ear tag, what was recorded and Change. A “Notes” row under an animal with a note.
  • Cattle not on the vaccination list: Added cattle [n] (always shown), Unidentified cattle [n] (only if reported), each added animal with its result and note.
  • Now submit your vaccination details: declaration, “Submit vaccination details”, “Save and continue later”.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • Every animal is listed, answered or not – no answer is shown as “—”.
  • Values: “Vaccinated”; a reason without “Not eligible:” (e.g. “Too young”, “Not at location”); “Vaccinated N months ago” from the record; a calf too young on the list but not given “Too young” reads “[n] days old, not presented”; “Animal was not presented” shows as “—”.
  • Is the job complete?: Yes when every animal has an answer – see screen 17.
  • Heavy line after every 5th animal (under its note if it has one); shared last 4 digits have a bar above and below.
  • Every Change link returns here after “Continue and save”. An animal’s Change opens screen 15 and comes back focused on its row.
  • Added cattle / Unidentified cattle Change open 13.
  • Back goes to 13 or 12.

Validation

None.

Data saved

On submit, a copy of the report’s answers – vax-reports – and the list it was made against is marked as reported.

Accessibility

To be worked out.

Vaccination list (V4)

/vax/vaccination-listv4?cph=…&list=N · “Download vaccination list” on 4, new tab

Purpose

The printable list the vet takes to the farm, with a slip at the end.

Elements

  • Page 1: farm, CPH, vaccination date line, cattle on this list, list created on, boxes for batch details. Then the table: No., tick box, Ear tag number (last 4 in bold), DOB, Sex, Breed, Notes.
  • Footer on each page: range of last 4 digits, page number.
  • A blank page for extra cattle, then the slip.

Where data comes from

To fill in.

Interaction rules

  • 35 cattle on page 1, 40 after. Heavy line every 5th row; shared last 4 digits barred.
  • A saved list always shows what it showed when it was made.
  • Notes: “[n] days old” for calves under 21 days; “Vaccinated [n] days ago” in the first month, then “Vaccinated [n] months ago” (to 12) for cattle vaccinated in the last year.
  • After a report: reasons read plainly (“Intended for export”, “Not at location”); cattle reported too young read “Reported too young ([date])” – dropped once 21 days old; cattle not presented have no note.
  • Cattle reported as vaccinated have their tick box ticked.

Accessibility

To be worked out.

  • Veterinary surgeon: name; veterinary practice / APHA office and telephone number; signature.

Interaction rules

  • Fills one A4 sheet, 10mm margins, no page number.

Test farm and data

Prototype: v1-7 runs on Pinecone Grove Farm (CPH 12/390/6810) – a made-up 407-cow dairy herd: 24 vaccinated in the last 12 months (on pages 2 and 6), 55 calves under 21 days at the bottom of the list (last 3 of page 9, all of 10 and 11).
