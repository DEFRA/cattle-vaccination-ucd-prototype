Vaccinate cattle for bovine TB

This service helps you prepare for bovine TB vaccination visits. You can also report completed vaccinations.

Use this service if you are:

  • an Official Veterinarian (OV) or qualified APHA officer
  • an approved tuberculosis vaccinator

You can use this service to:

  • access vaccination work assigned to you
  • prepare a list of cattle to vaccinate for bovine TB before the visit
  • report vaccinations you have completed
