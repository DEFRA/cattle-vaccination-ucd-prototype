How would you like to mark the cattle?

You can record what happened with each animal in whichever way is quickest for you. Choose based on what happened on your visit:

  • if you vaccinated most of the cattle on the farm, mark the vaccinated cattle first and then add a reason for any you could not vaccinate.
  • if you were only able to vaccinate a few of the cattle, mark the cattle that were not vaccinated first (and why) and the rest of the cattle will be marked as vaccinated.
