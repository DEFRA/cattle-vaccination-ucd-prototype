Vaccination data in Salesforce – prototype

V1

Business-need led

Explores how vaccination data is searched, checked, edited and viewed in Salesforce. This is based on a business need, not a user need — it has not yet been started from, or validated against, researched user needs. What's in the prototype:

  • Multi ear tag search — look up several animals at once by entering more than one ear tag number.
  • Vax checker — check the vaccination status of an animal or herd.
  • Edit vax data — amend or correct recorded vaccination details.
  • Vax history — view the vaccination history for an animal or herd.
