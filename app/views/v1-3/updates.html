V3

Skin tests (Mixed herds) – prepare a list and reporting

A redesigned skin test journey built for mixed herds where some cattle have been BCG vaccinated and some haven't. Separate SICCT and DIVA lists, a guided per-test reporting loop, a single screen for entering every reactor's measurements, automatic result interpretation, and printable lists designed to print exactly onto A4.

Everything in V2 is still here

The full vaccination and skin test journeys from V1 and V2 are still available. V3 keeps those flows and adds the changes listed below.

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Confirming the farm

New farm briefing

After picking a farm, vets see a richer briefing page covering the farmer's contact details, herd composition, last TB test, risk area, test interval, next skin test (with an "Overdue" tag when relevant) and the farm's vaccination programme.

Affects: /v1-3/confirm-herd-or-animal

Vaccination breakdown on the briefing New

The vaccination status row on the farm briefing now reads as two lines: how many cattle are vaccinated (with how many of those are overdue revaccination in brackets), and how many are unvaccinated. The "Vaccination booster" row has been folded into this single block, and "Last TB test day 2" has been renamed "Last TB test day 1".

Affects: /v1-3/confirm-herd-or-animal

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Separate SICCT and DIVA lists

Two lists for mixed herds, one for single-test farms New

Mixed herds now produce two separate printed lists – a SICCT list for the unvaccinated cattle and a DIVA list for the BCG vaccinated cattle – instead of a single combined list with an "Either" column. The animal's vaccination status determines which list it sits on, so the vet never picks between tests per row. Single-test CPHs render the one prepared list as before.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list · /v1-3/skin-test-list-order

"Which list do you want to format first?" picker New

A new picker page sits between the list-format choice and the printable list when both SICCT and DIVA were prepared. The vet picks SICCT or DIVA, formats and saves that list, then is sent back through the formatting flow for the other list. Single-test CPHs skip the picker.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list-order · /v1-3/skin-test-list · /v1-3/skin-test-list-confirmed

Single download for both lists New

When both lists have been formatted, the success page offers a single Download button that opens both stacks on one printable view, and the prepared-lists card on the farm tasks page shows one combined "SICCT and DIVA" entry instead of two separate links.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list-confirmed · /v1-3/farm-tasks

Page-1 entry table tuned per test New

The page-1 entry table now collects everything the vet needs to write in by hand. SICCT carries Day 1 date and time, Day 2 date and time, SICCT batch number, vaccine expiry date, diluent batch number and diluent expiry date. DIVA is single-visit so it carries Test date and time, DIVA batch number, vaccine expiry date, diluent batch number and diluent expiry date.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list

Top-right ear-tag range box on every sheet New

Every printable sheet now carries a boxed ear-tag range at the top right (and the bottom right) so the vet can identify each loose page at a glance, even if it gets separated from the cover sheet. The footer also carries the farm and CPH on the left and a "Page X of Y" indicator in the centre.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list · /v1-3/download-list

Choose how to view the list

Decide whether you want the list as a printable PDF, in a spreadsheet, or compatible with your handheld device.

Affects: /v1-3/list-format · /v1-3/list-spreadsheet · /v1-3/handheld-info · /v1-3/download-list

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Capturing test dates and times

Day 2 calculated automatically

Day 1 is the injection date. On the same screen the vet now records the start time of the test (hour, minute and AM/PM). Day 2 is calculated automatically as 72 hours after Day 1.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-date

Multi-day visits

If the test spans more than one day, the vet can tick a checkbox to reveal date and time fields for Day 2 so they can enter both dates manually.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-date

DIVA is a single-visit test New

DIVA only requires one visit, so the DIVA printable list has a single "Test date and time" column (no Day 2) and the measurement area collapses from Pre and Post to one Reading column. SICCT keeps its two-visit Day 1/Day 2 layout.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Reporting skin test results

No more "which test did you do?" question New

The system already knows whether the CPH was prepared for SICCT, DIVA or both, so the old "which test did you do?" page is gone. Single-test CPHs go straight to that test's batch-details page. Both CPHs see a "Which test do you want to record first?" radio picker before starting.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-type

Per-test batch and diluent details New

For each test the vet works through two confirmation pages: batch number plus vaccine expiry date, and diluent batch number plus diluent date. These values come from the printed list, so the pages are no-validation – the vet is just confirming what's already on paper.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-batch-details/:test · /v1-3/skin-test-diluent-details/:test

Per-test reactor and measurement loop New

Both CPHs now run the reactor pick + measurement table separately for each test, scoped to that test's prepared cattle. After the vet confirms one test, the journey loops back through batch-details for the other test. A new per-test confirmation page summarises batches and reactors before each loop completes.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-reactors-any · /v1-3/skin-test-reactors · /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table · /v1-3/skin-test-confirm-test/:test

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Identifying reactors

Clearer gate page

The decision page now reads "Did any cattle show a detectable reaction?" with a simple Yes / No. The previous "TB positive reaction" wording and the inconclusive-result hint have been removed.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-reactors-any

Select-all checkbox for the reactor picker

The "Identify the cattle" page has a select-all checkbox in the table header. Ticking it selects every animal in the table; the header stays in sync with individual row toggles so the vet can see at a glance whether some, all or none are selected.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-reactors

"Page N of M" panel dividers New

The reactor picker now groups cattle into the same 20 / 30 / 40 page chunks as the printed list, with a white card divider carrying an "H2 Page N of M" heading at every boundary. The vet can read the on-screen position against the loose printed pages they have in front of them.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-reactors · /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Recording measurements on one screen

All reactors in one table

Instead of stepping through one animal at a time, every reactor sits in a single tabular form. The vet can enter Day 1 and Day 2 measurements for every animal on one screen.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table

Avian and Bovine sites together

For SICCT rows the Day 1 and Day 2 cells split into Avian on top and Bovine underneath. DIVA rows show a single Reading column because DIVA is bovine only and single-visit.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table

Results worked out for you

The reaction description and overall result are calculated automatically as the vet types. SICCT uses an interpretation rules engine (clearly marked as a prototype and to be validated against the official TB64 matrix). DIVA labels the description "DIVA" rather than "Bovine" and treats any reading over 2 mm as a positive reactor; anything else is negative.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table

"From page N" dividers New

Each reactor on the measurement table is annotated with which page of the printed list it came from. The table groups reactors under "From page N" panel dividers, so the vet can quickly cross-reference their handwritten notes against the on-screen rows.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table

Save and continue later

A "Save and continue later" button on the measurement table saves what's been entered so far and returns the vet to the dashboard. They can pick the work up where they left off.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table · /v1-3/dashboard

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Finishing the visit

"Were all the cattle tested?" with reactor flags

The all-tested check page lists every animal with a red "Reactor" tag next to the cattle that have been marked as reactors.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-all-tested

Confirmation shows the test type

The review-and-submit screen shows Day 1 and Day 2 with the start time. When Day 2 was auto-calculated it adds a "Calculated as 72 hours after Day 1" note next to the value.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-confirmation

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Consistent tables across the service

Standard GOV.UK tables for browsing and selecting

Tables that aren't designed for paper printing now use the standard GOV.UK design-system table styling. The reactor picker, the not-tested picker, the all-tested check, the assign-cattle screens and the vaccinated-animal selector all read consistently.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-reactors · /v1-3/skin-test-untested · /v1-3/skin-test-all-tested · /v1-3/prepare-skin-test-assign-cattle · /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals

Same column order everywhere Updated

Every cattle table puts the ear tag first, then TB Vax, then the test-specific columns (eligibility, skin measurements), and finishes with DOB / Sex / Breed on the right and Notes at the far edge. SICCT lists drop the TB Vax column entirely because all those cattle are unvaccinated.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list · /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table · /v1-3/download-list · /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals

Easier-to-scan ear tags Updated

The full ear tag prints at the regular weight; only the last 4 digits are bold so the eye lands on the individual number. Duplicate ear tags now wrap the bold last-4 in a 4 px solid black top/bottom band (replacing the previous underline) so the duplicate flag reads consistently across every v1-2 table.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list · /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table · /v1-3/skin-test-reactors · /v1-3/skin-test-all-tested · /v1-3/download-list · /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals

Underlines that highlight what to check

The DOB cell underlines when an animal is around the 42-day testing age (between 35 and 49 days). The TB Vax cell underlines when the BCG vaccination sits in the "Revaccination due" window (over 46 weeks since vaccination but still within a year), so the vet sees the approaching deadline at a glance.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list · /v1-3/download-list · /v1-3/skin-test-diva-table

Cattle ordered by ear tag on the marking page

The page where the vet marks cattle as vaccinated always sorts the cattle by ear tag (last 5 digits) – the same default the prepared list uses – so the on-screen order matches the printed list.

Affects: /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Downloading and sharing lists

Success pages for ready-to-download lists

The "list is ready" pages for skin tests, vaccinations, the spreadsheet preview and handheld use a green GOV.UK success banner so the vet sees immediately that their list is ready. Each page leads with the primary action (Download) and offers a "Save to download later" link above the button.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-list-confirmed · /v1-3/download-list-confirmed · /v1-3/list-spreadsheet · /v1-3/handheld-info

Cross-linked alternative formats

Each ready-to-download page offers links to the same list in the other formats — PDF, spreadsheet or sent to a handheld — so the vet can switch format without rerunning the prepare-list journey.

Affects: /v1-3/download-list-confirmed · /v1-3/list-spreadsheet · /v1-3/handheld-info

Date created on prepared lists

Prepared lists on the farm tasks page show a "Date created" that matches the date printed on the list itself, so the vet can tell at a glance which version they're looking at when they come back to a list they prepared earlier.

Affects: /v1-3/farm-tasks · /v1-3/skin-test-list

{# -------------------------------------------------------- #}

Reporting BCG vaccinations

Five-state Eligibility column on the printed list New

The printed vaccination list shows each animal's eligibility position at a glance: Eligible (unvaccinated and 42+ days old), Eligible on DD/MM/YYYY (under 42 days old, with the eligible-from date), Vaccinated (within the last 46 weeks), Revaccination due DD/MM/YYYY (46+ weeks but still within a year) or Revaccination overdue (over a calendar year since vaccination).

Affects: /v1-3/download-list

Reshuffled printed vaccination list New

The printed vaccination list now shows a small tick column on the far left, the ear tag immediately to its right with no gap, then Eligibility and TB Vax flush against the ear tag, then DOB / Sex / Breed, and a writable Notes column on the far right. The page-1 entry table also captures vaccine expiry date, diluent batch number and diluent date alongside the existing batch and date fields.

Affects: /v1-3/download-list

Tighter selection page Updated

The marking page asks the simpler question "Which cattle were vaccinated?" and shows a live "You've selected X of Y cattle" counter as the vet ticks rows. The Summary card reads Total cattle / To do / Done, and validation has been removed so the vet can advance with whatever state they have. Page boundaries are shown as panel dividers every 20 (Easy) or 40 (Compact) rows so the on-screen view matches the printed list.

Affects: /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals

Cattle outcomes as one summary list on Check answers

The Check answers page shows vaccinated and remaining cattle (not found, deceased, withdrawn) together in one summary list, each row with its own "Change" link that deep-links straight to the relevant group on the marking page.

Affects: /v1-3/check-report-answers

"Check the cattle you've selected" review step

Between marking cattle on the select page and the final Check answers step the vet sees a "Check the cattle you've selected" review page that summarises how many cattle have been marked and how many are still to deal with.

Affects: /v1-3/select-vaccinated-animals · /v1-3/vaccination-marked-confirm

Declaration before submitting Updated

Before submitting the vaccination report the vet ticks "Yes, I have checked the answers" against a declaration that reads "I have accurately reported all the vaccinations." A short line below the radios — "This will send your vaccination report to Defra." — sits above the Continue button.

Affects: /v1-3/check-report-answers

Bug fixes and polish

"How do you want this list?" routes correctly

The three list-format options (PDF, spreadsheet, handheld) all route to the right page.

Affects: /v1-3/list-format · /v1-3/list-spreadsheet · /v1-3/handheld-info

Date examples on entry pages

Vaccine expiry and diluent expiry date fields now show a "For example, 05 2029" hint so the format is unambiguous at a glance.

Affects: /v1-3/enter-vaccine-batch-details · /v1-3/enter-diluent-batch-details

Trimmed the per-animal page

The DIVA and SICCT per-animal pages have been simplified – the reaction description, overall result radios and per-animal remarks have been removed because the result is calculated automatically.

Affects: /v1-3/skin-test-diva · /v1-3/skin-test-measurements

{# ------------------------------------------------------------ #}

Updates based on research

Round 2 of alpha research (May 2026) tested the V2 skin-test prototype with five vets in 45–50 minute semi-structured interviews. The mixed-herd anxiety, the desire for automation and the call for a tabular measurement screen drove most of the V3 changes.
