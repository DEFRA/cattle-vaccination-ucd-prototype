V4

Skin tests and vaccinations – prepare, report and amend

Building on V3: separate SICCT and DIVA lists for mixed herds, a guided per-test reporting loop, a tabular reactor-measurement screen with automatic interpretation, part tests and return visits, and recovery routes for the unhappy paths. The changes are grouped by journey below.

Find a farm

  • Richer farm briefing — contact details, herd composition, last TB test, risk area, test interval and next skin test (with an "Overdue" tag).
  • Vaccination breakdown — the status row shows how many cattle are vaccinated (overdue in brackets) and how many are unvaccinated.
{# ======================================================== #}

Prepare a list of cattle to vaccinate

  • Five-state Eligibility column — the printed list shows each animal as Eligible, Eligible on a date, Vaccinated, Revaccination due or Revaccination overdue.
  • Reshuffled printed list — tick column, ear tag, Eligibility and TB Vax, then DOB / Sex / Breed and a writable Notes column; page 1 captures batch, expiry and diluent details.
{# ======================================================== #}

Report BCG vaccinations

  • Tighter selection page — "Which cattle were vaccinated?" with a live "X of Y" counter, page dividers, and validation removed.
  • Record who was, or wasn't, vaccinated — record the vaccinated cattle, or just the exceptions with a reason and let the rest default to vaccinated; edit any group against the whole herd.
  • One summary list on Check answers — vaccinated and remaining cattle together, each row with a "Change" link to the right group.
  • "Check the cattle you've selected" review step — a summary of what's marked before the final check.
{# ======================================================== #}

Report completed vaccinations

  • Declaration before submitting — the vet confirms "I have accurately reported all the vaccinations" before the report is sent to Defra.
{# ======================================================== #}

Prepare a list of cattle for skin tests

  • Two lists for mixed herds — separate SICCT (unvaccinated) and DIVA (vaccinated) lists instead of one combined list; single-test farms get one list.
  • "Which list to format first?" picker — pick SICCT or DIVA, format and save, then loop through the other.
  • Single download for both lists — one Download button opens both stacks, and the farm page shows one combined entry.
  • Page-1 entry table per test — SICCT and DIVA each capture the right dates, batch numbers and expiry details to write in.
  • Ear-tag range box on every sheet — a boxed range top and bottom right; the cover is labelled, not numbered, so "Page 1" is the first cattle sheet.
  • DIVA is single-visit — one "Test date and time" column and a single Reading column; SICCT keeps Day 1 / Day 2.
{# ======================================================== #}

Report skin test results

  • The vet decides which test — the service recommends a test but the vet chooses SICCT, DIVA or both, since policy varies (for example DIVA may not be used in Wales).
  • Dates and times — Day 1 is the injection date and start time; Day 2 is auto-calculated as 72 hours later, or entered manually for multi-day visits.
  • Per-test batch and diluent details — confirmed per test from the printed list, then a per-test confirmation before looping to the other test.
  • Clearer "did any react?" gate — a simple "Did any cattle show a detectable reaction?" Yes / No.
  • Search, jump-to-page, select-all and a live count — shared on every selection screen: an ear-tag filter, "Go to a page" links, a select-all, and an "X of Y" count, with page size and sort at the top.
  • Reactors in a table, a page at a time — reactors are entered in a paged table, 10 at a time with "Save and continue" (and "Save and continue later").
  • Avian and Bovine together — SICCT rows split Day 1 / Day 2 into Avian and Bovine; DIVA shows one Reading; each reactor is tagged with its printed page.
  • Results worked out for you — reaction description and overall result are calculated as the vet types (SICCT via a prototype rules engine, to be validated against TB64).
  • Blank measurements can be submitted — leave Day 1 or Day 2 blank and still submit; those animals are captured as not fully tested, with a reason.
  • Not-tested cattle flagged for eligibility — too young, or tested elsewhere within 60 days, are flagged "Not eligible" so they aren't recorded in error.
  • Submit a part test and return for the rest — report the animals done; the remaining stay on the work list to reprint and report on a return visit.
  • "Were all the cattle tested?" with reactor flags — every animal is listed, with reactors tagged red.
{# ======================================================== #}

Report completed skin tests

  • Confirmation shows the test type — Day 1 / Day 2 with the start time and a "calculated 72 hours after Day 1" note; the submitted page reports reacted / not tested / negative counts and flags a part test.
{# ======================================================== #}

Download, print and share lists

These apply to both the skin test and vaccination lists.

  • Choose how to view — a printable PDF, a spreadsheet, or sent to a handheld.
  • Success pages — a green success banner, a primary Download action and a "save for later" link.
  • Cross-linked formats — switch between PDF, spreadsheet and handheld without redoing the list.
  • Date created on prepared lists — matches the date printed on the list itself.
{# ======================================================== #}

Work in progress and amending reports

  • Work in progress and recently completed — the dashboard splits work into resumable "in progress" and "recently completed", with status tags and "View all" pages.
  • Reopen and amend a completed report — reopen a skin test or vaccination report exactly as filed and correct it via the "Change" links.
  • Add cattle with breed autocomplete — add an animal that wasn't listed; the Breed field is a type-ahead autocomplete.
  • Designed for the unhappy paths — defined next steps for no results, the wrong holding, stale data, animals not found, lost pages, interrupted visits and post-submission corrections. We reviewed what could go wrong through four lenses — Recovery, User mistakes, Data quality and Operational reality — and mapped it out on the unhappy paths Mural.
{# ======================================================== #}

Across the service

  • Standard GOV.UK tables — consistent design-system tables on the browsing and selecting screens.
  • Same column order everywhere — ear tag, TB Vax, test columns, then DOB / Sex / Breed and Notes; SICCT lists drop TB Vax.
  • Easier-to-scan ear tags — only the last 4 digits bold; duplicates flagged with a black top/bottom band.
  • Underlines that highlight what to check — DOB underlined near the 42-day test age; TB Vax underlined when revaccination is due.
  • Marking page ordered by ear tag — matches the printed-list order.
{# ======================================================== #}

Bug fixes and polish

  • Populate a list for testing — a demo helper fills a printed list with realistic, handwriting-style data (a prototype aid, not part of the journey).
  • Added-cattle count fix — a leftover added animal no longer inflates the next report's herd count.
  • Reactor "Change" link fixed — the per-test confirm "Change" link now opens the reactor picker, not the empty measurement table.
  • List-format routing fixed — the PDF, spreadsheet and handheld options route to the right page.
  • Date examples on entry pages — "For example, 05 2029" hints on expiry fields.
  • Trimmed the per-animal page — removed the manual result radios and remarks now the result is automatic.
{# ======================================================== #}

Updates based on research

Round 2 of alpha research (May 2026) tested the V2 skin-test prototype with five vets in 45–50 minute semi-structured interviews. The mixed-herd anxiety, the desire for automation and the call for a tabular measurement screen drove the redesign that V4 builds on.
